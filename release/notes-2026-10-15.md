### Installation

With an already-unlocked bootloader, enter bootloader mode and confirm the
product is **flame**:

```sh
fastboot getvar product
fastboot boot OrangeFox-unofficial-flame.img
```

Enter the phone's PIN/password in recovery when prompted. After booting into
OrangeFox, select **Flash current OrangeFox recovery**, confirm, and wait for
successful completion before rebooting. For a temporary session, skip that
installation step and choose **Reboot → System** when finished.

Pixel 4 uses recovery in boot and has no separate recovery partition. Keep
the original boot image matching your installed ROM and a data backup.
For ROM ZIP installation, follow that ROM's own clean/dirty-install and A/B
slot instructions. Compatibility with other ROM/encryption combinations
requires its own testing.
